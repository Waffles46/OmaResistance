# Credits and asset provenance

## Omarchy

`Service.qml`, `LockView.qml`, and `PowerMenu.qml` derive from the author's local clone of Omarchy's `omarchy.lock`, reviewed against Omarchy 4.0.5-1. The upstream code is copyright David Heinemeier Hansson under MIT. Its notice is preserved in `OMARCHY-LICENSE` and the project `LICENSE`.

Upstream: <https://github.com/omacom/omarchy>

## Waffles46

Packaging, documentation, login-theme customization, and preview recordings are credited to Waffles46. Original contributions use MIT, matching the author's earlier Omarchy theme.

## Skull artwork

The braille-character skull is retained from the author's existing login/lock design. The maintainer identified [EmojiCombos — Skull ASCII Art](https://emojicombos.com/skull-ascii-art) as its source.

The original artist and an explicit reuse license for this exact artwork have not yet been verified. Credit to the source does not establish redistribution permission, and the project's MIT license does not establish a license for this third-party artwork. This uncertainty is disclosed for marketplace review.

The skull appears in both QML views, `preview.png`, and the two recordings. It has not been replaced or reshaped.

## Fonts and recordings

JetBrainsMono Nerd Font is an external dependency; no font files are bundled. Both GIFs are six-second recordings of the actual preview screens, with no password entered. `preview.png` is the reviewed lock-screen capture. No wallpaper photograph, film still, audio, or video clip is bundled.
